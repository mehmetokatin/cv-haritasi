MeoT Prep — AI-Assisted Math Practice App · Product owner & developer

Sep 2026 – Present

meot.com.tr · React Native (Expo), TypeScript, Supabase, Claude API · iOS, in App Store review

  • A friend who tutors math spent hours writing questions and testing numbers by hand. I designed each question as a template whose numbers change on every attempt, so students learn the method instead of memorizing answers.
  • Using the Claude API, I turned a photo of a question into a template whose wrong options come from mistakes students commonly make.
  • Defined teacher–student flows: assignments, timed mock exams scored with YKS net rules, step-level error diagnosis, a mistake notebook that re-asks missed questions with new numbers, rankings and topic analytics.
  • Built with trust in mind: parental consent for under-18s (KVKK), Row Level Security, rate limits and 35 automated tests in CI. Next step: a pilot with 5 teachers and 50 students by January 2027.
